Excel Online
Pop-Up Blocker

This Extension suppresses annoying pop-ups in Excel Online.
🛈
Only dialogs contained in iframes with origins matching "https://usc-excel.officeapps.live.com/*", "https://excel.officeapps.live.com/*", or "https://officeonline.sfcollab.org/*" are suppressed.
To expand functionality to the overall page or to additional origins, please contact the developer.

Select which pop-ups to block below!
Any modifications to the Excel Online experience are made solely at the user's discretion. Please use responsibly.

Pop-Up Text

Button Label
🛈
Exact text on the button that will be automatically clicked. The extension will look for a button with aria-label matching this. Most X's have aria-label="Close".
